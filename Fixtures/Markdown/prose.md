# A Quiet Preview

Markdown previewing should feel calm. A writer should be able to open a file, place the preview beside an editor, and forget that the preview is a separate app until it becomes useful.

## Reading Rhythm

Good typography does not call attention to itself. It gives paragraphs enough room to breathe, keeps line length comfortable, and makes headings easy to scan.

Blockquotes should have a distinct voice:

> The preview is the product. Every other control supports the document.

## Export

When the document is ready, exporting should feel like a natural continuation of previewing rather than a separate technical ritual.
